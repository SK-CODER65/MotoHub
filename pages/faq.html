Frequently asked questions.

Quick answers about orders, delivery, returns and your account.

Orders & payment

How do I place an order?

Add products to your cart, open the cart and select Continue to checkout. Fill in your delivery details and place the demo order.

Which payment methods are available?

This demo store shows Cash on Delivery and UPI options. No real payment is ever taken.

Where can I see my orders?

Open Orders in the navigation bar. Orders are saved in this browser only.

Delivery & returns

Is delivery free?

Delivery is free on orders above ₹3,000. Smaller orders have a flat ₹99 delivery charge.

How long does delivery take?

In a real store, delivery would usually take 3–7 working days depending on your PIN code. This demo does not ship products.

Can I return a product?

Our demo policy allows returns of unused gear within 7 days. Contact us with your order ID to start a return.

Products & sizing

How do I pick the right helmet size?

Measure around your head just above the eyebrows and compare with the brand's size chart. Read our helmet guide for more tips.

Are the products safety certified?

Product listings are sample content. Always check the actual certification of any safety gear before purchase.

Account

Do I need an account to shop?

No. You can browse, save a wishlist and place demo orders without an account.

Is my password secure?

Demo accounts are stored in your browser's localStorage and are not secure. Never use a real password.
